### Is OpenGolfSim really free?

OpenGolfSim will always be free to install and play. This includes our practice range, a fun corn-hole game, and a challenging 9-hole course. Additional courses are available for purchase.

### Which devices / platforms can I install OpenGolfSim on?

OpenGolfSim is available for [download]({{ site.external_urls.download }}) on MacOS, Windows, iOS, and Android.

### What are the minimum requirements?

OpenGolfSim requires a device with WebGPU or WebGL 2.0 support. Requirements assume a display resolution of up to 1080p.

| Platform | Low | Medium | High |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Windows 10+ (64-bit)** | Intel UHD 620, 8 GB RAM | Intel Iris Xe, 8 GB RAM | NVIDIA RTX 2060 or equivalent, 16 GB RAM |
| **macOS 12+** | Intel Mac (2017+), 8 GB RAM | Apple M1, 8 GB RAM | Apple M1 Pro or later, 16 GB RAM |
| **Android 7+** | Lenovo Tab M9 or equivalent, 3 GB RAM | Snapdragon 865 or equivalent, 4 GB RAM | Snapdragon 8 Gen 2 or equivalent, 6 GB RAM |
| **iOS / iPadOS 16.4+** | A12 chip (e.g. iPad 8th gen) | A14 chip (e.g. iPad 10th gen) | M1 chip (e.g. iPad Air 5th gen) |

If your device can't keep up, OpenGolfSim automatically lowers its render resolution. You can adjust your resolution and quality level each time you launch a game.


### Why do I need to create an account?

You'll need an OpenGolfSim account to store purchase history and launch monitor preferences and handle syncing over multiple devices.

### Can I build my own courses?

Yes. You can build your own custom courses in minutes with our custom [course building tools](/course-building/).
